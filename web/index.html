How it plays

One touch. Every decision is when you let go.

Hold and release

Hold to grow the ring, release to burst it. A bigger ring reaches further, but while it grows you aren't attacking, and the shapes keep coming.

PERFECT

Let go while a shape sits on the edge: double damage, a spark that jumps to its neighbours, slow motion, and a combo that multiplies your score.

30 upgrades

Burst shapes drop gems; level up and pick 1 of 3: Echo, Frost Ring, Chain Lightning, Ricochet, Satellites and more. Some pairs unlock synergies.

8 worlds, 48 stages

Each world has its own colours and music and one new shape: dashers, shielded wardens, splitters, orbiters, hatchers, phantoms, brutes. Every stage ends with a boss.

The Forge

Every run pays coins, win or lose. Buy permanent upgrades, ring types that change how you play, and starting perks.

Daily Run and Endless

A new Daily Run every day, the same for everyone, with its own twist. Or see how long you last in Endless. No pop-up ads, ever.
