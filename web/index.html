How it plays

One touch. Every decision is when you let go.

Hold and release

Hold to grow the ring, release to burst it. A bigger ring reaches further, but while it grows you aren't attacking, and the shapes keep coming.

PERFECT

Let go while a shape sits on the edge: double damage, a spark that jumps to its neighbours, slow motion, and a combo that multiplies your score.

One task per level

Every level shows its task before it starts: burst shapes, land PERFECTs, survive, build a combo or beat a Giant. Each takes 15 to 45 seconds.

8 worlds, 320 levels

Each world has its own colours and music and one new shape: dashers, shielded wardens, splitters, orbiters, hatchers, phantoms, brutes. Every world ends with a boss.

Three stars

Every shield you still have when the task is done is a star. Clear a level without a scratch for all three, and collect awards along the way.

Fair about ads

No pop-up ads, ever, and nothing while you play. Videos only when you ask for one: a revive or an hour's try of a theme or ring style.
