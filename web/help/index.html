Playing Ringburst

The basics

You are the glowing core in the middle. Hold anywhere on the screen and a ring grows outward from the core; let go and it bursts outward as a shockwave that hits every shape inside the ring. The faint dashed circle shows how big the ring can get. While the ring grows you aren't attacking, so a big ring is a gamble: more reach, but the shapes get closer.

A shape that touches the core breaks one of your shields (the blue pips around the core). Lose them all and the run is over. You can revive once per run with a short video or 150 coins.

PERFECT

When a shape sits right on the ring's edge, the ring turns white, the shape gets a white halo and a soft blip plays. Let go then for a PERFECT: the whole burst hits twice as hard, a spark jumps to nearby shapes, time slows for a moment and your combo goes up. The combo multiplies your score up to ×4. A burst that hits nothing breaks the combo, and so does a hit on the core.

Gems and upgrades

Burst shapes drop gems; your waves pull them in. Fill the XP bar under the score to level up, and the run pauses so you can pick 1 of 3 upgrades. There are 30, from Echo (a second wave) and Frost Ring to Chain Lightning, Ricochet and Satellites. Four synergies only appear once you own both halves: Shatter, Resonance, Supernova and Inferno. Upgrades last for the run.

Worlds, stages and bosses

There are 8 worlds of 6 stages. Each world brings one new shape, shown before its first stage: dashers lunge, wardens carry shields that only a PERFECT breaks, splitters burst into minis, orbiters spiral, hatchers park far away and breed, phantoms fade out of reach, and brutes are armoured. When the bar at the top fills, the boss arrives: a Guardian on stages 1–5, the world's boss on stage 6. Beat it to clear the stage and open the next.

Stars

Each stage has three stars, each its own goal: clear the stage, take no hits, and reach the stage's combo target. Stars from different runs add up, and each new star pays 10 coins.

Coins and the Forge

Every run pays coins, win or lose, more for higher scores. Spend them in the Forge on permanent upgrades (an extra shield, more damage, a faster or bigger ring, a wider PERFECT window and more), on ring types that change how you play (Twin, Glacier, Lens, Nova, Cinder, Titan), and on starting perks. Choose your ring and perk on the stage card before you play.

Daily Run and Endless

The Daily Run is a new run every day, the same for every player, with one twist (for example Swarm, Giants or Precision). It is made on your device from the date, so it works offline. Monday is the gentlest and Sunday the hardest. Finish it to earn the daily reward and keep your streak. Endless opens after Neon Shore stage 3: every shape, a boss every two minutes, and it keeps getting harder.

Shop (Android)

Everything opens by playing. The shop sells optional extras: the Supporter pack (no banner ads, and revives and double coins without videos), colour themes, ring styles, coin bags, and a tip jar. Themes and ring styles can be tried free for an hour with a video. Purchases go through Google Play; use Restore purchases after reinstalling.

Sharing

After a run, tap Share on the result card to send a picture of it with a link to the game, or Save it to your gallery.

Saving

Progress saves automatically on your device. In the browser, progress is stored by your browser for this site; clearing site data erases it. Nothing is uploaded.

Controls

Touch or mouse: hold and release anywhere. Keyboard: hold and release Space; 1–3 pick an upgrade; Esc pauses or goes back. On Android, the Back button pauses a run and works everywhere else. Screen shake and vibration can be turned off in Settings.
